Dragon Aspect Flight 1.8.1 - generated flight animation stack
============================================================

The candidate contains 1,167 player-only, flight-only OAR HKX aliases across
eleven equipment families. Equipment families use OAR's root scope for both
sexes. The neutral fallback also includes a male scope because some animation
stacks request Animations\male\MT_* and action paths directly.

Neutral and equipment locomotion
--------------------------------

All ordinary locomotion and idle originals use the credited Flying Mod Beta
flight idle rather than a falling or grounded-walking clip:

  Flying_Mod_Idle.hkx
  SHA-256 21FD87DF7C0D5D9718183944576DC5510A580D530FBD88D0ECF8C36B3DB5F451

Only the fallback family carries generic mt_* locomotion. Equipment families
carry their requested 1hm_*, 2hm_*, 2hw_*, dw*, bow_*, crossbow_*, mag_*, and
staff* originals. A separate WeapTypeQtrStaff family overrides the general
two-handed route at priority 2147483610.

Aerial attacks
--------------

Vanilla and recognized MCO melee attack originals map to the corresponding
credited NickNak aerial normal/power clips. The same names remain available
while descending; DAF no longer consumes attack input in that state.

Block, bash, draw, sheathe, and shout
------------------------------------

These action originals are not static poses. Each generated clip keeps its
source upper-body tracks, duration, binding, and annotation events, while its
root and lower-body tracks are sampled from Flying_Mod_Idle.hkx. This preserves
weapon placement, block/bash timing, and shout release without grounded legs.

The quarterstaff route overrides seven action originals with composites made
from Animated Armoury's open-permission quarterstaff animations. Its normal
2hw_blockbash uses the compatible vanilla source because Animated Armoury's
normal bash donor uses an unsupported interleaved representation. Dawnguard's
crossbow shout offset has no lower-body tracks and is intentionally left as an
upper-body overlay.

Optional modlist aliases are also flight-scoped. Maxsu's weapon/shield block-hit
originals resolve to the matching family composite, and Dynamic Bow Animation's
xpe0_* draw/equip originals resolve to the matching bow composite. Those mods
remain optional; the aliases are dormant when their custom originals are not
requested.

Magic and staff
---------------

Magic and staff action originals continue to use the credited xp32/Neumeria
clips with flight lower-body tracks. Their successful v1.8.0 routing was kept.
