Dragon Aspect Flight 1.8.0 - generated flight animation stack
============================================================

The release contains 899 player-only, flight-only OAR HKX aliases across ten
equipment families. Equipment families use OAR's root scope for both sexes. The
neutral fallback also includes a male scope because some installed animation
stacks request Animations\\male\\MT_* paths directly.

Neutral and equipment locomotion
--------------------------------

All locomotion, idle, block-idle, bash-intro, and attack recovery paths use the
credited Flying Mod Beta flight idle:

  Flying_Mod_Idle.hkx
  SHA-256 21FD87DF7C0D5D9718183944576DC5510A580D530FBD88D0ECF8C36B3DB5F451

Only the fallback family carries generic mt_* locomotion. Equipment families
carry their own requested 1hm_*, 2hm_*, 2hw_*, dw*, bow_*, crossbow_*, mag_*,
and staff* originals, preventing grounded Stances/MCO locomotion without
duplicating the generic set ten times.

Aerial attacks
--------------

Vanilla and MCO melee attack originals map to the corresponding credited
NickNak aerial normal/power clips. Directional left-side aliases are
byte-identical where NickNak shipped one animation for both directions.

Magic and staff
---------------

Magic and staff action originals use the credited xp32/Neumeria clips. Weapon
draw/sheathe, bow release, crossbow release/reload, and shout transitions are
not replaced with static poses because their annotations drive gameplay.
