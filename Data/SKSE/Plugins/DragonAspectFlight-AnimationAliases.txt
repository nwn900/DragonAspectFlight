Dragon Aspect Flight 1.8.3 - generated flight animation stack
============================================================

This reviewed static tree contains 1,045 player-only, flight-only OAR HKX
aliases across eleven equipment families. Equipment families use OAR's root
scope for both sexes. The neutral fallback also includes a male scope because
some animation stacks request Animations\male\MT_* and action paths directly.

Ownership boundary
------------------

DAF deliberately does not own ordinary `mco_attack1.hkx` through
`mco_attack10.hkx`, or any draw/equip/unequip/sheathe commit original. Those
slots remain with the installed behavior stack and the OAR rule owner that
controls the active weapon graph. DAF retains vanilla/non-MCO attacks,
`mco_powerattack*` and other MCO special attacks, block/bash actions, and
flight locomotion. This is a static ownership correction, not a gameplay
compatibility claim.

Neutral and equipment locomotion
--------------------------------

All ordinary locomotion and idle originals use the credited Flying Mod Beta
flight idle rather than a falling or grounded-walking clip:

  Flying_Mod_Idle.hkx
  SHA-256 21FD87DF7C0D5D9718183944576DC5510A580D530FBD88D0ECF8C36B3DB5F451

Only the fallback family carries generic mt_* locomotion. Equipment families
carry their requested 1hm_*, 2hm_*, 2hw_*, dw*, bow_*, crossbow_*, mag_*, and
staff* originals. The Greatsword family is also the explicit aerial fallback
for OAR types 5 and -1, proven two-handed pike/spear/halberd/nodachi keywords,
and optional active Spear of Skyrim/Spear of Omicron forms at priority
2147483605; real type-6/10 axes remain in Base 50 at 2147483604. A separate
quarterstaff OR (WeapTypeQtrStaff, WeapTypeQuarterstaff,
OCF_WeapTypeQuarterstaff2H) overrides that fallback at priority 2147483610.
Shield-only equipment (OAR type 11) is routed through Base 20 so shield
block/bash originals retain a flight-scoped block profile.

Aerial attacks and actions
--------------------------

DAF routes its vanilla/non-MCO melee attack originals and recognized MCO
power/special originals to credited NickNak aerial clips. Block, bash, and
shout action originals keep their source upper-body tracks, duration, binding,
and annotation events while their root and lower-body tracks are sampled from
Flying_Mod_Idle.hkx.

The quarterstaff route overrides its block/bash action originals with
composites made from Animated Armoury's open-permission quarterstaff
animations. Its normal 2hw_blockbash uses the compatible vanilla source
because Animated Armoury's normal bash donor uses an unsupported interleaved
representation. It does not own draw, sheathe, equip, or unequip originals.

Optional modlist aliases are flight-scoped. Maxsu's weapon/shield block-hit
originals resolve to the matching family composite. These aliases are dormant
when their custom originals are not requested.

Magic and staff
---------------

Magic and staff action originals use credited xp32/Neumeria clips with flight
lower-body tracks. Coverage metadata records the xp32/Neumeria source names
that remain aerialized, plus overlapping ground-locomotion names deliberately
mapped back to the root-stable flight donor in `basePoseOverrideNames`.
