Dragon Aspect Flight 1.7.0 - third-party animation attribution
================================================================

The bundled aerial-combat animation clips are from:

  Jumping Attack
  Author: NickNak
  Nexus: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/68043

NickNak's published permissions allow redistribution, modification, conversion,
and use of the assets in other mods with credit. Dragon Aspect Flight bundles
the direct NickNak animation and landing-effect assets only. It does not claim
authorship of those assets.

The original Jumping Attack package supplies 81 of the 96 NickNak paths
referenced by its behavior source. Dragon Aspect Flight supplies the remaining
15 directional/left-side paths as byte-identical aliases of the nearest
licensed NickNak clip. The exact alias mapping is recorded in:

  SKSE\Plugins\DragonAspectFlight-AnimationAliases.txt

SHA-256 hashes for every bundled HKX and NIF are recorded in:

  SKSE\Plugins\DragonAspectFlight-AnimationHashes.txt

Dragon Aspect Flight does not bundle JumpAttack.esp, Nemesis/Pandora source,
or generated behavior graphs. Install Jumping Attack and generate its behavior
patch for the user's own behavior stack.
