Dragon Aspect Flight 1.6.0 - third-party animation attribution
================================================================

The bundled aerial-combat animation clips are derived from:

  NickNak's Jumping Attack
  Author: NickNak
  Nexus: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/68043

NickNak's published permissions allow redistribution, modification, and use of
the assets in other mods with credit. Dragon Aspect Flight bundles only the
NickNak animation subset from the installed donor package. It does not bundle
the donor package's ESP files, Papyrus scripts, generated behaviors, Mid Air
Shouts implementation, or other authors' core flight animations.

The regular attack aliases retain NickNak's original transforms and
hit/Precision annotations. The dedicated power-attack aliases preserve the
original transforms and embed the hit/Precision timing that the donor supplied
through its behavior patch. MCO aliases also add combo, recovery, power-chain,
and attack-stop annotations using the open-source serde-hkx/hkxc serializer:

  serde-hkx
  Project: https://github.com/SARDONYX-sard/serde-hkx
  License: Apache-2.0 OR MIT

Exact source and generated SHA-256 hashes are recorded in:

  meshes\actors\character\animations\OpenAnimationReplacer\
  Dragon Aspect Flight - Flight Combat\animation-manifest.json
